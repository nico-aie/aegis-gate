Set up two-factor

Protect your account with an authenticator app.

  1. Open Google Authenticator (or Authy / 1Password) on your phone.
  2. Tap + and scan this QR code.
  3. Enter the 6-digit code the app shows to activate.
Can't scan? Enter the setup key manually
